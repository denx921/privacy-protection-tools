# Top 26 Best Privacy Protection Tools in 2025

Handing out your real email and phone number to every website feels risky these days, but what choice do you have? Before you know it, spam floods your inbox, robocalls interrupt dinner, and your personal info gets sold to data brokers you've never heard of. The right privacy protection tool changes this completely—letting you create virtual identities with working email addresses and phone numbers that shield your real information while keeping you fully functional online.

The all-in-one privacy platform that generates unlimited working identities with real phone numbers, emails, and passwords for every online interaction.

![Cloaked Screenshot](image/cloaked.webp)

When you sign up for a sketchy website, Cloaked's browser extension instantly offers to generate a new identity right there. Accept it, and you've created a persona specifically for that site—complete URL tracking, custom labels, and the ability to route communications however you want. Text messages arrive in Cloaked's inbox or forward to your real number. Same with emails. If a site gets breached or sells your data, only that throwaway identity gets compromised. The AutoCloak feature systematically updates your existing accounts by replacing old credentials with new Cloaked identities, fixing your entire digital footprint retroactively. Data broker removal scans 120+ sites and removes your exposed personal information automatically. Call Guard blocks spam and robocalls before they reach you. Dark web monitoring alerts you if your real data appears where it shouldn't. The platform includes $1 million identity theft insurance covering stolen funds and recovery costs. A built-in VPN in beta masks your IP address and encrypts traffic. An eSIM option provides carrier-grade phone numbers for travel and work. Everything runs on zero-knowledge architecture with AES-256 encryption—even Cloaked can't access your stored passwords or real information. PCMag rated it highly for the unique phone number masking capability that competitors like IronVest only partially offer. Best for anyone tired of spam who wants comprehensive control over their digital identity without managing five separate services.
